# View Conversations

Converse makes reading and navigating your chat history clean, structured, and visually rich. Here's what you can expect when viewing your conversations:

## Navigating Chat History

* **Message History & Organization:** Scrolling up smoothly loads your older past messages. Your history is neatly divided by clear **day separators** to help you find chats from specific days. Helpful **unread indicators** also let you know exactly where you last left off.

![Day Separator Example](/img/02-chat-basics/02_03_01.png)

* **Message Versions:** Notice a message was edited? You can easily view the message's edit history to see previous versions of the text.

* **Interface Customization:** Depending on your setup, the chat layout can utilize custom message templates to give you a personalized viewing and searching experience.

## Identifying Participants

* **Avatars & Sender Info:** Who said what? Every message is clearly labeled with the sender's display name and their avatar (profile picture). 
* **Group Chat Roles:** In multi-user group chats, user roles (like Admin or Moderator) are often displayed alongside the sender's name so you immediately know who is speaking.
* **Mentions Highlighting:** In group chats, if someone mentions your name directly, the message is automatically detected and highlighted so you never miss an important ping.

## Rich Media & Message Types

* **Distinct Message Types:** Converse intelligently differentiates various types of messages. You'll see distinct visual styles for normal 1:1 messages, group chat messages, system info/errors, live file-upload progress bars, and notices for retracted (deleted) or moderated messages.
* **Inline Media Embeds:** Share a link to a picture or video? Converse will automatically display inline images, audio players, and video embeds right in the chat stream. It also supports "unfurl cards," which give you a smart preview of shared web links without having to leave the chat window.

*(Note: While emojis are fully supported for composing, reaction display features are currently rolling out and may not be active yet in all chats.)*
