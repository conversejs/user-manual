# Send a Message (1:1 Chat)

Converse provides a rich and incredibly powerful messaging experience for your direct 1:1 chats. Whether you're sending a quick "hello" or writing a detailed update to a contact, you have all the tools you need to make your point.

## Composing Your Thoughts

* **Drafts & Multi-line Text:** Take your time typing. If you accidentally close a chat, Converse's **draft autosave** ensures you won't lose your work. You can also press <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd> to type on multiple lines.
* **Message Limits:** The handy **message-length indicator** lets you know when you're approaching the server's text limit.
* **Send Controls:** Send your message by either pressing <kbd>Enter</kbd> on your keyboard or clicking the configurable **Send** button in the chat toolbar.

## Express Yourself

* **Emojis:** Open the emoji picker to search through a massive library of emojis. You can choose preferred skin tones and use dropdown shortcuts to quick-insert them as you type.
* **Text Formatting:** Use rich text styling (like bold or italics) to add visual structure to your messages.

## Sharing Files & Links

* **Attachments:** Need to send a document or image? Use the file chooser to select and upload single or multiple files securely over HTTP.
* **Media Previews:** When you paste a URL into the chat, Converse will automatically generate an inline preview card (unfurl) for websites, images, and audio/video links, so your contact can see what it is before clicking.

## Managing Sent Messages

* **Citing / Quoting:** Keep conversations organized by using the quote (cite) feature. It will quote the specific message you are referencing, placing it right above your text.
* **Edit & Correct Typos:** Made a spelling mistake? No problem! Use the inline edit mode to correct any message you've already sent.
* **Spoiler Alerts:** Sharing the ending of a great movie? Send your text as a **Spoiler message**, which hides the text visually until your contact explicitly clicks to reveal it.

_Note: Multi-User Chat (MUC) specific features, such as user mentions and highlight detection, are covered in the group chat section._
