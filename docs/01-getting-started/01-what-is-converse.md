# What is Converse?

Converse is a powerful, open-source, and web-based XMPP chat client that brings complete messaging freedom. It is the leading messaging solution for developers, communities, organizations, and businesses needing a secure and customizable chat platform.

![Converse Chat](/img/01-getting-started/01_1_converse-overlayed.png)

## Why Choose Converse?

Converse provides a feature-rich chat experience that can be seamlessly integrated into any website with just a few lines of JavaScript:

- **Flexible Deployment:** You can use Converse as a standalone full-page app, embed it as a widget, or use it as an overlayed chat on your current website.
- **Top-Tier Security:** Keep your conversations private with end-to-end encryption (OMEMO) and manage communities with powerful moderator tools.
- **Feature-Packed:** Enjoy direct messages (DMs), group chatrooms, file sharing, typing indicators, read receipts, and message archiving. It also supports chat statuses and desktop notifications.
- **Accessibility:** Converse is translated into over 45 languages so users all over the world can communicate easily.
- **Developer Friendly:** With a plugin-based architecture and no vendor lock-in, Converse is highly customizable. It integrates seamlessly with any XMPP server.

Whether you're looking for a simple embedded chat or a full-scale messaging application, Converse has everything you need to keep your community connected.
