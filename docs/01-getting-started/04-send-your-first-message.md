# Send Your First Message

Now that you're securely logged into Converse, it's time to start chatting! The best part is that you don't even need to add someone as a contact first—you can jump right into a conversation.

## 1. Get Your Receiver's JID

Because XMPP is a federated network, you can chat with anyone on any provider! To do this, you just need to know their **Jabber ID (JID)**. Just like an email address, their JID will look something like `friend@example.com`. Make sure you have their JID ready.

## 2. Start a Chat Directly

To send your first message immediately:

1. Look for the **Start a chat** option or search icon in your Converse interface.
2. Type or paste the receiver's full JID directly into the field.
3. Select their JID, and Converse will instantly open a new chat window with that person.

You don't need to wait for a contact request to be accepted—you can reach out to them right away!

## 3. Send Your Message

Once the chat window is open:

1. Type your message into the text box at the bottom.
2. Press **Enter** on your keyboard (or click the send button).
