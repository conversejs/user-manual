# Log in to Converse

Once you have your XMPP account details (whether provided by your company or claimed on a public provider), you are ready to log in and start chatting.

![Login Screen](/img/01-getting-started/login.png)

## Entering Your Details

To access your account, simply follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Jabber ID (JID):** On the Converse login screen, type in your full chat address. This includes both your username and your provider, combined just like an email address (for example, `yourname@example.com`).
2. **Type your password:** Enter the secret password associated with your account.
3. **Log In:** Click the **Log in** button to connect.

If your details are correct, Converse will securely connect you to your provider and load your contact list and recent conversations.

> **Note:** If you are logging into a company-provided account, your IT administrator or organization will have provided you with your exact JID and password.
