# Access the Chat

To start chatting on Converse, you need an XMPP account. If you don't already have one, don't worry—getting one is fast, free, and works a lot like getting an email address.

## Choosing a Provider

Unlike centralized chat apps (like WhatsApp or Telegram), XMPP is decentralized. This means no single company owns the network. Instead, there are many different servers (or **providers**) out there that host accounts. 

You can browse a list of free, public XMPP providers at [providers.xmpp.net](https://providers.xmpp.net/). When you pick a provider, your new chat address (often simply called a **JID**, or Jabber ID) will look just like an email address. For example, if you pick the provider `example.com`, your address could be `yourname@example.com`.

## Selecting the Provider in Converse

Once you have decided on a provider, you can enter it directly in the Converse chat window:

![Provider Selector](/img/01-getting-started/01_2_provider_selector.png)

## Registering Your Account

Some providers allow you to register directly from the Converse chat interface. After picking your provider and choosing to register a new account, follow the on-screen prompts to choose a username, enter your desired JID, and set a password:

![Account Registration](/img/01-getting-started/registration.png)

Congratulations! Once registered, you will be automatically logged in and ready to start chatting.
